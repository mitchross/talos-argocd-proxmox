# Open WebUI and Intercept recovery

Argo creates replacement PVCs on `longhorn-wired-ha`, requesting two copies on separate eligible hosts. Existing Kopiur Restore objects populate them from the latest backup before binding.
The original backup identities stay unchanged, and existing Kopiur schedules automatically protect the replacements. Missing backups fail the restore instead of binding empty volumes.
Original failed PVC specs and data remain protected with `Prune=false,Delete=false`; removing them is not a rollout requirement.
Keeping those declarations also provisions unused originals on a fresh cluster: 30 GiB of single-copy reservation alongside 60 GiB for the replacements, plus up to 30 GiB for backup staging.
The backups verified on September 10 were captured at 03:05 UTC for WebUI and 22:01 UTC for Intercept, leaving possible gaps of nearly 19 hours and about 2 minutes before the fault. Future restores select latest rather than a permanently fixed date.
Completion is visible in Kopiur Restore status, PVC binding, application readiness, replica health, and subsequent scheduled backup results.
